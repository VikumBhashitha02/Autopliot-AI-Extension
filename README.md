## 🧸 What is AutoPilot AI? (Explained so simple, even a 7-year-old gets it!)

Imagine you are building an amazing Lego castle on your computer. 

Every single minute, a little popup box appears on your screen asking:
> *"Are you sure you want to add this block? Click YES!"*  
> *"Do you want to accept these color changes? Click ACCEPT!"*  
> *"Do you want to run this safe command? Click OK!"*

Clicking "Yes", "OK", and "Accept" hundreds of times a day is boring and makes your fingers tired! 🥱

**AutoPilot AI is your smart, friendly robot helper living right inside your code editor.** 🤖✨

Whenever a safe popup or code change review appears, AutoPilot AI smiles, inspects it with its smart magnifying glass, and clicks the recommended **"Yes"** or **"Accept all"** button for you in the blink of an eye!

Now you can just sit back, relax, build cool projects, and let your robot friend do all the repetitive clicking!

## 📥 How to Install AutoPilot AI (In 4 Easy Steps!)

### Method 1: The Easy Click Way
1. Open **VS Code** or **Google Antigravity IDE**.
2. Press `Ctrl + Shift + P` on your keyboard (or `Cmd + Shift + P` on Mac) to open the magic search bar at the top.
3. Type: `Extensions: Install from VSIX...` and hit **Enter**.
4. Choose the file named **`autopilot-ai02-1.0.0.vsix`**.
5. 🎉 That’s it! Look on the far-left sidebar—you will see the shiny new **AutoPilot AI** icon!

### Method 2: The Command Line Way (For Ninjas!)
Open your terminal and paste one of these lines:

```bash
# For Visual Studio Code
code --install-extension autopilot-ai02-1.0.0.vsix

# For Google Antigravity IDE
antigravity --install-extension autopilot-ai02-1.0.0.vsix
```

## 🎮 How to Use AutoPilot AI — Step-by-Step Guide

Using AutoPilot AI is as easy as playing a video game. Here is how to use it in 5 simple steps:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 1: Click the AutoPilot icon on the left sidebar  │
│                           ▼                            │
│  STEP 2: Pick your Robot Mode (Safe, Fast, or Ask-Me)  │
│                           ▼                            │
│  STEP 3: Pick your Speed (Instant, Fast, or Chill)     │
│                           ▼                            │
│  STEP 4: Press the BIG GREEN "Start" button! ▶️        │
│                           ▼                            │
│  STEP 5: Sit back and watch the robot do the work! ✨  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 1: Open the Robot Sidebar
Look at the left-hand strip of your editor. Click on the **AutoPilot AI** icon. The friendly dashboard will open up!

### Step 2: Pick Your Robot’s Personality (The 3 Modes)
Click on the **Mode** dropdown to choose how you want your robot to behave:

| Mode | What it means | When to use it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 **Safe Auto-Pilot** *(Recommended)* | The smart guardian! It only clicks safe, recommended buttons and asks for your permission on anything unusual. | **Best for everyone!** Perfect for daily coding and beginners. |
| 🚀 **Full Autonomous** | Super fast autopilot! Automatically accepts all valid prompts and code diffs without interrupting you. | Best when you are doing big repetitive tasks and want max speed. |
| 🙋 **Manual Approval** | The polite helper! It notices prompts, highlights the best button, but waits for you to say "Go ahead!". | Best when you want to inspect every single step yourself. |

### Step 3: Choose How Fast the Robot Moves
Pick your reaction speed:
- ⚡ **Instant (100ms):** Super Sonic speed! Clicks almost instantly.
- 🐇 **Fast (250ms — *Recommended*):** Quick, smooth, and human-like.
- 🚶 **Comfortable (750ms):** Gives you time to see what popped up before the robot clicks it.
- 🐢 **Deliberate (1500ms):** Nice and slow so you can observe every detail.

### Step 4: Click the Big Green "Start" Button ▶️
Click **"Start Monitoring"** on your screen (or press `Ctrl+Shift+P` and choose `AutoPilot AI: Start Monitoring`).
- The status light will turn **Bright Green (ACTIVE)**.
- Your robot is now awake and on guard!

### Step 5: What if I want it to STOP right now? 🛑
Don't worry! You are always in 100% total control:
- Click the **Emergency Stop** button at the top of the sidebar.
- Or press the universal keyboard shortcut: **`Ctrl + Alt + Shift + E`** (on Mac: **`Cmd + Alt + Shift + E`**).
- Everything immediately freezes, all active workflows stop, and the robot goes to sleep instantly.

## 🛡️ Safety Shield: Why AutoPilot AI is 100% Safe

Your computer and your code are precious. That is why AutoPilot AI has built-in **SafetyGuard Ironclad Armor**:

1. 🚫 **Dangerous Buttons are Permanently Locked Out:**
   The robot is physically programmed to **NEVER** click buttons like:
   - `Delete`
   - `Reject`
   - `Discard changes`
   - `Don't Save`
   - `Cancel`
   Those buttons are strictly reserved for human fingers only!

2. 🧼 **Harmful Command Blocker:**
   If any command tries to run something scary like `rm -rf /` or `format c:`, the SafetyGuard blocks it instantly before it can touch your computer.

3. 🙈 **Secret Redactor:**
   If you work with passwords, API keys, or tokens, the robot automatically blurs them out in all logs so nobody can ever peek at them.

4. 🏠 **100% Private (No Cloud Spying):**
   Everything stays right on your own machine. AutoPilot AI never uploads your code, your prompts, or your data to any remote server or third party.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Does this cost any money?
**A:** No! It is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license.

### Q: Do I need to create an account, register, or enter my email?
**A:** No! There are zero logins, zero passwords, and zero accounts. It works immediately as soon as you install it.

### Q: Will AutoPilot AI delete my code or files?
**A:** Never! AutoPilot AI's SafetyGuard permanently disables any automated clicking on destructive buttons like `Delete`, `Discard`, or `Reject`. It only accepts safe, recommended changes that you want approved.

### Q: Can I use this on both VS Code and Google Antigravity?
**A:** Yes! AutoPilot AI automatically detects whether it is running inside Visual Studio Code or Google Antigravity IDE and adjusts itself seamlessly.

### Q: How do I turn it off quickly?
**A:** Just hit `Ctrl + Alt + Shift + E` or click the big red **Stop** button in the top bar.
